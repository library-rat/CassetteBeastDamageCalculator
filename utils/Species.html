In Cassette Beasts, each member of the player's team takes the form of a monster species in battle.

When fighting, the player can choose to record an opponent's monster using a tape, expanding the set of monsters they can use in battle. Up to six species can be actively swapped between; any additional species reside in the tape inventory and can be selected when resting.
